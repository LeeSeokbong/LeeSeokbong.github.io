Bandi, That's Not What I Meant

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Package name com.kingstar.bandi

Korean · English · 日本語 · 繁體中文

Leo Pocket (the "Operator") operates the mobile game "Bandi, That's Not What I Meant" (the "App"). In accordance with Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, we explain how we handle your personal information.

At a glance

  • There is no signup or login.
  • Your conversations with Bandi, the words you teach, and Bandi's memories are processed and stored only on your phone and are never sent to the Operator.
  • The AI model (about 2.6GB) is downloaded from Hugging Face only if you choose to download it (by default, on Wi-Fi only). During the download, your IP address is visible to Hugging Face and its delivery network (CDN).
  • For rewarded ads, Google AdMob collects device information such as the advertising ID.
  • The App sends anonymous usage records (a random device number made by the App, the app version, which screens and features were used, and when) to the Operator's statistics server. It does not send your name, account, location, conversations, or save files, and you can turn this off in Settings > Anonymous usage stats.
  • The only information the Operator receives is anonymous usage records (which you can turn off), an AI text report that you send yourself (received through a Google Form inside the App) and an inquiry email. We do not ask for your name or email address when you report.

3. Provision to third parties and entrusted processing

The Operator does not provide your personal information to third parties. However, the companies involved receive information directly through the advertising SDK in section 4 and the model download in section 5.

Entrusted processing and overseas transfer (Google Forms): To receive AI text reports without a server, the Operator uses Google Forms from Google LLC and, if necessary, Google Sheets. Reports are stored in the Operator's Google account, and Google stores and processes them on the Operator's behalf. This may constitute entrusted processing under Article 26 and overseas transfer (entrusted processing and storage) under Article 28-8 of the Personal Information Protection Act, so we notify you as follows.

  • Recipient (transferee): Google LLC (contact: support.google.com/policies)
  • Entrusted tasks: providing the report form and storing report content
  • Destination countries: the United States and other countries where Google data centers are located
  • Date and method of transfer: sent over an encrypted network (TLS) when you open and submit the form from the in-app report screen
  • Items: the report items in section 2 (reported text, reason, note, app version, time of report). When the form is opened, general connection information such as IP address, device and browser information, and cookies is also passed to Google.
  • Retention period: same as section 2 (1 year after receipt)
  • How to refuse: if you do not report or do not agree to send, nothing is transmitted, and it does not affect your use of the game.

The form opens with the report items already filled into its address, so that content is passed to Google at the moment the form is opened. It is saved to the Operator's response list only when you press "Submit", and if you close it before submitting, the Operator does not receive it. How Google handles connection information follows the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Entrusted processing and overseas transfer (Cloudflare): Anonymous usage records are sent to and stored on a statistics server the Operator runs on Cloudflare, Inc. services (Cloudflare Pages and D1).

  • Recipient: Cloudflare, Inc. (contact: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy)
  • Entrusted task: receiving and storing usage records
  • Countries: the United States and other countries where Cloudflare data centers are located
  • When and how: records are gathered while you use the App and sent over an encrypted network (TLS), for example when you leave the App
  • Items: the anonymous usage records in Section 2. Your IP address is visible to Cloudflare during transmission, but the Operator does not store IP addresses.
  • Retention: 90 days after receipt
  • How to refuse: turn it off in the App's Settings > Anonymous usage stats. The App then stops sending records and deletes the random device number on your phone. This does not affect gameplay.

4. Ads (Google AdMob), including overseas transfer

The App includes the AdMob advertising SDK from Google LLC. Ads are rewarded ads that play only when you choose to watch them by pressing a button. The advertising SDK starts not when the App launches but when the ad button first appears, and from then on the information below may be sent to Google even while ads are being prepared. In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, Google's consent management tool (User Messaging Platform) first shows a consent screen.

  • Recipient: Google LLC (contact: support.google.com/policies)
  • Destination countries: the United States and other countries where Google data centers are located
  • Date and method of transfer: sent over an encrypted network (TLS) when ads are requested and shown while the App is running
  • Items: advertising ID, app set ID, IP address, in-app ad interactions (launches, taps, video views), diagnostic information (launch time, unresponsive rate, etc.)
  • Purpose: serving and measuring ads, analytics, preventing fraud
  • Retention period: according to the Google Privacy Policy
  • How to refuse: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads. If you delete your advertising ID, it is not used for personalized ads. You can progress in the game without watching rewarded ads.

Personalized ads (behavioral information): Google may use your advertising ID and app usage information to show interest-based ads. The Operator does not directly collect or store behavioral information.

How Google uses information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Downloading the AI model (Hugging Face)

The first time you run the App, it asks whether to download the AI model. If you choose to download it (or press download later in the App's Settings > Bandi's Words), the App downloads the AI model file (about 2.6GB) from a public repository of Hugging Face (Hugging Face, Inc., USA). As with ordinary internet communication, your IP address and an app name string that does not identify your device ("Bandi-Android") are passed to Hugging Face and its delivery network (CDN), and they may be logged under those companies' policies. The Operator does not receive these logs. By default, downloads happen on Wi-Fi only, and you can play without downloading. While downloading, the App keeps downloading even if you close it, showing a "Bandi is learning to talk" notification (Android's WorkManager, a data-sync foreground service), and if the connection drops it resumes from where it left off when Wi-Fi is connected. If you press [Stop] in the notification or settings, it stops downloading. Whether you are on Wi-Fi or mobile data and whether you have enough storage are checked only on your phone and are not sent anywhere.
Hugging Face Privacy Policy: huggingface.co/privacy

6. Backup (Android Auto Backup)

Game save files and App settings may be stored in your Google account backup (Google Drive) through Android's Auto Backup feature. Google keeps this backup in your account, and the Operator cannot see it. The AI model file is not backed up. You can turn this off in your phone's Settings > Google > Backup.

7. Sharing you do yourself

  • Sharing a mishap postcard (image): When you press the share button, the Android share sheet opens and the image is passed to the app you choose. The image carries an "AI-generated" label.
  • Friend Bandi code: A text code containing Bandi's name, level, evolution form, personality, title, and belief cards (including the sentences you taught). It leaves your phone only when you copy or share it. Before sending it to a friend, please check that you did not put personal information in a belief sentence.
  • Exporting a save file: Saved as a JSON file to a location you choose in Settings.
  • Exporting saved reports: Report records are saved as a JSON file to a location you choose in Settings.

Information passed on this way is subject to the policies of the app or service you choose.

8. Notifications

Game notifications, such as when the bag is full, are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No external server is used for notifications, and you can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

9. Destruction of personal information

  • Information on your phone: deleted immediately when you uninstall the App or delete the App's data.
  • Reports: when the retention period ends or you request it, they are deleted without delay from the Google Form responses and the linked spreadsheet.
  • Inquiry emails: when the retention period ends or you request it, they are permanently deleted from the mailbox without delay.

10. Your rights and how to exercise them

You may request access, correction, deletion, and suspension of processing of your personal information held by the Operator (report content and inquiry emails). Send an email to the contact below and we will handle it without delay (within 10 days). Reports contain no name or contact information, so when you request access to or deletion of a report, please tell us the reported text and the approximate time of the report. You can check and delete the information on your phone yourself in the App.

11. Children under 14

This App is intended for users aged 16 and over. The Operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14, and if we learn that a report or inquiry came from a child under 14, we will delete it without delay.

12. Security measures

The Operator does not store personal information such as names or contact details on a server. Anonymous usage records are viewed only on a statistics page that only the Operator can log in to. Reports (the spreadsheet linked to the Google Form responses) and inquiry emails are kept only in the Operator's account with two-step verification turned on, and are not shared with anyone else, so that no one other than the Operator can access them. The App's save files are kept in Android's app-private storage, where other apps cannot read them.
